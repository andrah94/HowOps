Website terms

Updated September 26, 2026

These terms describe use of the HowOps website. HowOps is the consulting brand led by Andra Howard, MA. Questions can be sent to contact@howopsconsulting.com.

Website information and inquiries

This website introduces HowOps services and examples of its work. Sending a form, emailing, or booking an introductory call does not create a paid engagement or reserve a project start date.

Proposals and paid work

Services are custom-scoped. Deliverables, fees, payment terms, timing, responsibilities, software costs, and any continuing support are set out in a separate proposal or agreement. That agreement governs the engagement. Retainer scope and fees are agreed before ongoing support begins.

Examples and client comments

The animated journey illustrates how work can move through a system. It is not a live demonstration of a connected client account. HQ builds are tailored to their organizations. Featured testimonials describe an individual client’s experience; they do not establish a standard result for every project.

Responsible use
